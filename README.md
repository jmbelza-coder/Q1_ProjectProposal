# BaonBalance

## About the Project

BaonBalance is a simple program that is designed to helps scholars manage their canteen budget. It calculated the total cost of selected food and drinks and checks whether the user has enough money to afford them.

## Problem Statement

Scholars tend to lose track of their spending when buying something from the canteen. This leads to them to spend more than their available budget, worse case scenario they have insufficient money to pay for what they bought.

## Project Objectives

- Calculate the total cost of a scholar's selected items based on their quantity and price.
- Determined whether the scholar' budget is enough to pay the needed amount or cost.
- Display the remaining balance of the scholar or the amount of money they need if the budget is insufficient.

## Planned Features

- Enter available budget
- Enter food/drink prices
- Enter their quantity
- Calculate their total cost
- Check the budget
- Display remaining balance or needed amount

## Planned Inputs

- Available budget
- Amount of Items
- Item name
- Item price
- Item quantity

## Planned Outputs
- Total cost
- Remaining balance
- Amount needed if the cost exceeds the budget
- Budget status
